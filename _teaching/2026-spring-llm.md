This workshop introduces the architecture of Large Language Models (LLMs) and explains how to leverage them effectively and responsibly in text-intensive research. It covers alternative computational methods for text analysis, strategies for evaluating AI models for specific research tasks, techniques for ensuring reliable AI-generated outputs, and best practices for citing AI-powered methodologies.
