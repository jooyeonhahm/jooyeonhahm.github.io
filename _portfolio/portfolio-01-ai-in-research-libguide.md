This library guide offers researchers a practical framework for evaluating, applying, and citing Large Language Models (LLMs) in text-based research. It offers a structured overview of LLM architecture, outlines alternative computational methods for text analysis, and provides step-by-step guidance on evaluating AI models for specific research tasks. Geared towards researchers who are seriously considering the application of LLMs in their text-intensive research, it also addresses strategies for ensuring the reliability of AI-generated output and best practices for citing AI-powered research methodologies. By thoroughly understanding the capabilities and limitations of AI technology, researchers can achieve more insightful analysis results and enhance their workflows.
